Selection

Paint or outline the areas that may be sorted. Hold Alt to subtract. With nothing selected, the whole image is sortable.

Edge mode
Edge softness

Fades the sort out toward the edge of the selection and the edges of each path's width, so it blends into the untouched image.

Outer edge max
Inner edge max

Each row of the sort starts and stops a random distance from the selection edge: up to the outer amount beyond it, or up to the inner amount inside it. Reshuffle in the Sort panel changes the pattern. Edges that run parallel to the path are not affected.

Subject

Preserve a subject over the sorted image. Edge detection is used to detect the subject. The first use downloads a ~10 MB helper.

Pick the Subject tool (S), then draw around the edges of the subject. Use the Keep and Remove brushes to fix mistakes.
